# AI Solutions

Building real-world AI products that solve actual problems.

## Active Products

### Hazeon MCQ Engine
AI-powered multiple choice question generator for competitive exam preparation (UPSC, HCS). Uses Gemini API to generate 8 different MCQ types with formatted Word document output.

### Hazeon AI Interviewer
AI mock interview system for UPSC/HCS candidates. Practice with AI, get real-time feedback, improve your answers.

### Instagram AI Pipeline
Automated content creation and scheduling for Instagram — from idea generation to posting.

## Philosophy

> **Build AI products that people actually need, not AI features that sound impressive.**

The best AI products are invisible — they make existing workflows faster, cheaper, or more accessible. Nobody cares about the model behind the curtain. They care about the result.
